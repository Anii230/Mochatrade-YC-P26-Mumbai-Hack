## Overview

**Mochatrade** is a next-generation trading platform that gives global (non-US) retail traders , starting with India , seamless, leveraged access to US equities, indices, commodities, and select pre-IPO names through **perpetual futures**.

It solves a long-standing structural gap: the world’s deepest and most liquid equity market has historically been closed off to sophisticated retail traders outside the United States by friction in brokerage accounts, cross-border remittances, leverage restrictions, settlement delays, and tax complexity.

Mochatrade removes those barriers. Traders fund in local currency (UPI in India), go long or short with leverage, trade 24/7, and settle back into rupees — without ever opening a US brokerage account or triggering traditional LRS/FEMA remittance processes at the user level.

The platform is built for serious derivatives traders who already understand futures & options (F&O) and want the same experience applied to the most important assets on the planet: Apple, Nvidia, Tesla, the S&P 500, gold, and more.

---

## The Problem

India is the largest retail derivatives market in the world by volume, accounting for roughly **60% of global equity derivatives activity**. More than **140 million** Indians hold demat accounts. Yet most of these traders remain locked out of meaningful, leveraged exposure to US equities.

Existing options fall short:

- **Traditional US stock platforms** (Vested, INDmoney, etc.) — long-only, no leverage, T+2 settlement, wire delays, LRS limits, and heavy tax paperwork.
- **Offshore CFD brokers** — opaque pricing, custody risk, and regulatory uncertainty.
- **Crypto-native perpetuals venues** — designed for crypto audiences, not for Indian F&O traders who want to trade Apple the way they trade Nifty.

Mochatrade was built to close this gap with a clean, compliant, INR-native product.

## Product

### What You Trade
Contracts that track the price of US stocks, indices, and commodities — structured as perpetual futures (no expiry). You never hold the underlying share. You trade the price, long or short, with margin posted in rupees.

### Key Capabilities
- **50+ US stock perps** including NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, META, GOOGL, AMZN, MSFT, and more
- **Indices, commodities** (gold, silver, oil), crypto, and select pre-IPO names
- **Up to 20× leverage** (higher figures have been referenced in early materials; current product messaging emphasizes professional-grade leverage)
- **24/7 markets** — trade any hour, any day, including weekends
- **Instant UPI funding & same-day withdrawals** in INR
- **INR-margined** positions with transparent margin, square-off levels, and costs shown before confirmation
- **Advanced tooling** — TradingView charts, fundamentals, analyst consensus
- **Self-custodial architecture** with regulated on-ramps/off-ramps
- **In-app tax reporting** designed for Indian residents

### How It Works (High Level)
1. **Tokenized / price-tracking exposure** to US equities and other assets
2. **Perpetual futures** for leverage and continuous trading
3. **Regulated INR rails** (FIU-registered partners) for seamless on-ramp and off-ramp

Users deposit INR via UPI, post margin in rupees, trade, and withdraw profits or losses back to their bank — all without cross-border remittance friction at the individual level.

## Regulation & Compliance

Mochatrade is registered with the **Financial Intelligence Unit – India (FIU-IND)** as a **Virtual Digital Asset Service Provider (VDASP)** and is designed to operate within the Indian regulatory framework for VDA derivatives.

- VDA derivative trading is legal in India
- No LRS/FEMA restrictions are triggered at the user level for platform activity
- Perpetual futures profits are treated as ordinary business income (not the flat 30% VDA tax + 1% TDS that applies to certain other digital assets)
- Full digital KYC (PAN + government ID)
- Tax reports generated in-app

Always consult a qualified tax advisor for personal circumstances.
